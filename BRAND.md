# Project You+ Brand System

**Tagline:** A better you, every day.

**Brand promise:** Your AI-powered operating system for a healthier, wealthier, and more intentional life.

## Identity
- Product name: **Project You+**
- Official mark: geometric **Y+** from `public/project-you-mark.svg`
- Wordmark: **Project You+** with **You+** in the primary purple accent
- UI typeface: **Inter** — 300 / 400 / 500 / 600 / 700
- Personality: calm, premium, intelligent, direct, supportive, action-oriented
- Brand phrase: **DISCIPLINE CREATES FREEDOM**

## UI rules
- Purple is reserved for brand identity, primary actions, active states, progress, and high-value AI moments.
- Use dark neutral surfaces and thin borders; avoid random blue/purple hues outside the approved system.
- Radius system: `4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24px`.
- Spacing system: `4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48px`.
- Cards use subtle depth; modals use medium elevation; overlays use high elevation.
- Charts stay minimal with purple lines/bars and semantic colors only when meaning requires them.
- Motion is subtle, smooth, intentional, and state-driven.
- Icons are minimal, rounded, purposeful, and visually consistent at ~2px stroke.
- Mobile primary tap targets are at least 44pt.
- Core actions must not depend on hover.

## Product language
- **1% Score** — overall progress across life areas
- **Your 1%** — highest-leverage daily improvement
- **Run My Day** — AI-generated daily plan
- **Focus Areas** — core life categories
- **Personal Blueprint** — long-term vision, goals, and priorities
- **Weekly Review** — AI-powered progress summary
- **Project You+ Insight** — AI-generated observation
- **Project You+ Action** — recommended next step
- **90-Day Mission** — current improvement cycle

## Voice
Clear. Supportive. Motivational. Intelligent. Practical. No fluff.

The AI Coach should be insightful, direct, encouraging, personalized, and action-oriented — closer to a high-performance chief of staff than a motivational chatbot.

## Brand statement
We believe in the power of consistent, intentional improvement. Project You+ gives you the clarity, structure, and AI support to build a healthier, wealthier, and more fulfilling life — one percent better, every day.
